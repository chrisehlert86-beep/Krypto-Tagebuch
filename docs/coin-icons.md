# Coin artwork

ETH and SOL logos are bundled with the APK and work without network access.
They identify the respective assets; they do not imply project endorsement.

- Ethereum: original black diamond PNG from https://ethereum.org/assets/,
  https://ethereum.org/_next/static/media/eth-diamond-black.31u_5ih2w7osr.png.
  The image is unchanged and displayed on a white circular background for contrast.
- Solana: native VectorDrawable conversion of the official SVG geometry and
  gradient, with original proportions, path and color stops preserved:
  https://solana.com/branding and
  https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-com/blob/main/apps/web/public/src/img/branding/solanaLogoMark.svg.

Bitcoin's existing orange symbol and the app launcher icon are unchanged.
